### 1. How top PMs increase their leverage with AI

Source: Lenny's Newsletter

#### Summary

Colin Matthews frames PM AI adoption as three ladders of leverage: personal leverage for finishing your own work, product leverage for closing the gap between an idea and shipped software, and systems leverage for repeatable AI-assisted workflows. The personal ladder moves from AI-written text to generated artifacts to full to-do items connected to tools like Amplitude, Google Drive, Notion, and Canva; his examples include PRDs, A/B-test analysis, support-ticket review, and a generated cost model comparing self-hosted agents with managed providers at 5M+ annual agent instances. The product ladder moves from web prototypes to code-based prototypes in a real codebase to agent-created pull requests, using a Stride cancellation/subscription-status flow as the concrete example of testing product judgment against real components and implementation constraints.

#### Why It Matters

For North America AI PM coaching, this is a clean diagnostic ladder: clients can identify whether their portfolio shows text assistance, artifact creation, real workflow completion, or product-shipping leverage. For AI Ops, it supports a practical sales frame for non-technical owners: stop buying generic AI prompts and instead connect AI to the tools, files, and review loops where actual work gets finished.
