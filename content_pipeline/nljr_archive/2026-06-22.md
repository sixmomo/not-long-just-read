### 1. How Claude Mythos found a 15-year-old bug in Mozilla Firefox | Brian Grinstead

Source: Lenny's Newsletter
URL: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-claude-mythos-found-a-15-year
Published: 2026-06-22

#### Summary

Mozilla built an agentic bug-finding pipeline around Claude Mythos and pointed it at Firefox's enormous codebase. Instead of asking one agent to inspect everything, the system first scores and ranks files, gives agents tightly scoped goal loops with clear pass/fail signals, and uses verifier subagents to reject false positives. Human engineers still review every patch. The pipeline contributed to 423 security fixes in one month, including a bug introduced 15 years ago; Brian Grinstead attributes roughly half the result to the model and half to the harness.

#### Why It Matters

This is a strong example of why valuable AI products are workflow systems, not model demos. The reusable product lesson is to combine task decomposition, prioritization, evaluation, verification, retry loops, and human approval. You can use this case to show prospective AI PM clients what a compelling portfolio project should demonstrate beyond simply calling an LLM API.
